# RoomSync Roadmap — Becoming Essential, Then Profitable

## Why it can become a daily habit (essential)

The loop already exists: **meals → groceries → expenses → settle**, plus owner
staff-task tracking. Depth beats breadth from here:

1. **Rent reminders** — notify members before the owner-set billing day, and
   nudge overdue dues weekly. Turns the app into the household's money clock.
2. **Published mess menu** — owners/chefs publish the week; residents check it
   every morning (the highest-frequency surface in a PG).
3. **Month-end auto-report** — “Your September: spent ₹4,320 across 31 items,
   settled ✓”. Shareable, builds trust, brings users back monthly.
4. **Join-request push** — owners currently must open Settings to see pending
   requests; notify them (email/push) or approvals stall and groups die.
5. **Receipt capture** — photo → amount/date prefilled on the expense form.
   Removes the #1 friction in expense logging.
6. **Recurring templates** — rent, Wi-Fi, maid salary auto-created each billing
   day instead of re-typed.

## How it can earn (in order of fit)

1. **PG SaaS subscription (best lever).** Owners already run rooms, staff,
   tasks, and billing here. Charge per bed/month (e.g. ₹49/bed) past a free
   threshold (~10 members): billing automation, staff payroll tracking,
   month-end collection reports. Owners have money; students don't — sell to
   owners.
2. **In-app rent/dues collection.** Roommates pay rent and settle dues via UPI
   inside the app with auto-receipts and owner approval. Revenue via a small
   convenience fee or float — the dues ledger becomes a payment rail.
3. **Staff payroll add-on.** Salaries and advances for cooks/maids tied to
   completed tasks; flat monthly fee per PG.
4. **Freemium friends groups.** Core splitting stays free (acquisition);
   charge for CA-ready export statements, receipt scanning, and priority
   support.

## Suggested build order

Billing-day reminders → month-end reports → recurring templates → PG
subscription paywall (member cap + billing automation) → UPI collection pilot
→ payroll. Each step reuses the roles, tasks, and dues systems already built.
