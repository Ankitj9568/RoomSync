Who Is It For

Roommates

Split groceries and expenses, plan meals, and settle up with the fewest possible payments.

PG & Flat Owners

Allot rooms, assign cooks, maids, and watchmen their daily tasks, and track monthly rent collection with two-step login security.

Staff

Cooks, maids, and watchmen see exactly today's work — meal headcounts and assigned tasks — and tick them done.

PG, Flat, or Friends Group

PG

Owner-run paying guest. The owner allots rooms, assigns staff for the whole PG, and sets the monthly rent and bill day.

Flat

A rented flat where members run daily chores and choose their own help, while the owner controls rent and billing.

Friends

A pure roommate group with no owner — everything shared equally, like splitting ₹10,000 among 10 friends.
